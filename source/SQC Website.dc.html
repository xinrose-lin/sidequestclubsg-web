WHY WE EXIST

Creating a place where your ideas outside the 9-5 find its life tooo!

We want to give young adults the structure, accountability and community to ship their side quests — despite the 9 to 5.

WHY SIDE QUESTS STALL

NO STRUCTURE

The idea's big and vague, so it never becomes a task you can actually start and sustain. It just stays an idea.

NO ACCOUNTABILITY

No deadline, no one expecting to see it. So nothing happens whether you do it or not.

NO COMMUNITY

Nobody around you is building anything either. It stays a private hobby, and private hobbies die quietly.

And so the idea gets pushed to “later” — but later never comes…

THE ROADMAP · ONE COHORT

Nine weeks. Five meets. Your idea's v0 shipped.

Week by week, your idea finds its shape — and you discover new paths of your own :)

  1. WEEK 1

    Scope the quest

    A guided design sprint: think big, envision at scale, then cut it down to a v0. Set the goal you'll be held to for the next nine weeks!

    DESIGN SPRINT GOAL SETTING
  2. WEEK 2

    Pitch to the party

    Five minutes to share your what and why: with people who want you to win. You leave with sharper direction and a party that knows your quest!

    PITCH FEEDBACK
  3. WEEK 3

    Meet the legends

    Guided sessions and sharings from those who've shipped their own side quests — the wins, the stalls and how they got through!

    GUEST SPEAKER FIRESIDE CHATS
  4. WEEK 4

    From grind to gleam

    A workshop on finding your first audience, building in public, and getting your v0 showcase-ready.

    SHOWCASE PREP
  5. WEEK 5

    Quest day !!

    Not a pitch competition — but the day to celebrate your idea's birthday! Share the joy, the sweat and tears, at an open exhibition space with friends and strangers alike :)

    PUBLIC SHOWCASE

THE PARTY

Built for people with a full-time job and a restless idea.

STRUCTURE

A nine-week arc, with a meet every two weeks; so the quest has a start and an actual end!

ACCOUNTABILITY

Regular ketch-ups, with feedbacks and sharings to keep your idea going; and a final showcase to mark its fruition!

COMMUNITY

Under 15 per cohort, so everyone knows what everyone is building!
