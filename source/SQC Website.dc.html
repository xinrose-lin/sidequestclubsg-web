WHY WE EXIST

Creating a place where your ideas outside the 9-5 find its life!

We want to give young adults the structure, accountability and community to ship their side quests — despite the 9 to 5.

WHY SIDE QUESTS STALL

NO STRUCTURE

  • Ideas can be big and vague
  • No clear way to start

NO ACCOUNTABILITY

  • No deadline, no one waiting to see it
  • Life gets busy, other things fill the calendar

NO COMMUNITY

  • Nobody around you is building
  • Private hobbies die quietly

And so the idea gets pushed to “later” — but later never comes…

THE ROADMAP · ONE COHORT

Nine weeks. Five meets. Your idea's v0 shipped.

Week by week, your idea finds its shape — and you discover new paths of your own :)

  1. WEEK 1

    Scope the quest

    • Design sprint: dream big, cut to a v0
    • Set the goal you'll be held to
    DESIGN SPRINT GOAL SETTING
  2. WEEK 2

    Pitch to the party

    • 5 mins: your what and why
    • Leave with sharper direction + a party behind you
    PITCH FEEDBACK
  3. WEEK 3

    Meet the legends

    • Talks from people who've shipped their own
    • The wins, the stalls, the way through
    GUEST SPEAKER FIRESIDE CHATS
  4. WEEK 4

    From grind to gleam

    • Find your first audience, build in public
    • Get your v0 showcase-ready
    SHOWCASE PREP
  5. WEEK 5

    Quest day !!

    • It's your idea's birthday!!
    • Share the pride and joy :))
    PUBLIC SHOWCASE

THE PARTY

Built for people with a full-time job and a restless idea.

STRUCTURE

  • 9 weeks, a meet every two
  • A real start and a real end

ACCOUNTABILITY

  • Regular ketch-ups + feedback
  • A final showcase to mark it

COMMUNITY

  • Under 15 per cohort
  • Everyone knows what everyone's building
